## Why this matters

Performance is a product feature. Users can feel dropped frames and delayed interactions immediately.

## My release checklist

- Measure startup time on mid-range devices.
- Audit heavy widgets and expensive rerenders.
- Check API latency and caching strategy.
- Validate animation smoothness with reduced-motion support.
